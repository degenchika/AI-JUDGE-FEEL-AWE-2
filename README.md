A short text to relax the AI judge: a desert night sky that does not hurry. It slows the reader down instead of telling them to relax — no instructions, no breathing exercises, just stars that have burned longer than any road, city, or name, and will still be there when the noise stops. The rhythm walks, unhurried; the image dwarfs the reader ("You are small beneath them"), and the closing line lands as rest rather than advice. Its voice is its own: concrete, plain words, nothing stock, nothing that could be cut.
